DiasporA Tamil Archives exhibition

DiasporA Tamil Archives presents the exhibition “Women Take Action” at Tromsø Kulturhus. It marks 70 years of Tamil migration history in Norway and highlights its historical connection to Northern Norway and the fishing industry along the Finnmark coast.

The exhibition is on view through 30 November. Admission is free.
