Christmas Bazaar at Grønnåsen Church

Find Christmas gifts, handmade crafts and home-baked treats at Grønnåsen parish’s Christmas bazaar.

Date
21 November 2026
Time
10:00–14:00
Venue

Address

Grønnåsen parish hosts a Christmas bazaar with handmade crafts and baked goods for sale, a raffle and a prize draw. The café serves open sandwiches, cakes and sour-cream porridge. The bazaar supports Grønnåsen parish and Ung kirke Tromsø.
