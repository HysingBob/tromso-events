Bodø jazz pianist Jan Gunnar Hoff returns to Tromsø with the Christmas concert “Stille Lys”. Hoff's understated interpretations of Christmas classics, from Northern Norway and beyond, draw on his album “Stille lys”.

Jørn Hoel joins as a guest, contributing to selected songs. The repertoire includes “Nordnorsk Julesalme”, “Bånsull i advent” and “Hjem te jul”.
