A Christmas concert with Tønes and Ingvar Hovland

Christmas songs, everyday dilemmas and gentle humour with Tønes and Ingvar Hovland.

Frank “Tønes” Tønnesen and Ingvar Hovland have created Christmas shows together for 20 years. In this new concert performance, they explore the small and big questions of Christmas—from where to celebrate and gift-giving to decorating the tree and cold floors—with songs, humour and reflection.

Sigfrida and Johanne also appear.
