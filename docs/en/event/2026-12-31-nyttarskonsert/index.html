New Year's Concert at the Arctic Cathedral

Cathedral organist Thorsten Ahlrichs presents a musical firework display on the Arctic's largest organ.

Date
31 December 2026

21:00
Venue
Ishavskatedralen
Address

Price
350 kr (inkl. 25 kr gebyr)

Cathedral organist Thorsten Ahlrichs performs a 45-minute programme of Christmas brilliance, virtuosity and musical fireworks on the Arctic's largest organ. The programme includes Bach's Toccata and Fugue in D minor, The Bell Carol, German, Norwegian and French Christmas music, and Camille Saint-Saëns' Danse macabre. Thorsten Ahlrichs gives a short introduction to the music before the concert.
