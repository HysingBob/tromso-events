Morten Abel comes to Tromsø Kulturhus with the Full of Dreams tour. The concert features highlights from the English-language album Full of Dreams, favourites from his whole career and the big songs that made him one of Norway's most loved artists.

Tickets are sold through Kulturhuset. According to the organiser, the concert is almost sold out.
