Ordkalotten: Poetics seminar with Kristin Vego

A morning seminar on writing a novel that is not yet finished, with the author Kristin Vego.

Archived event

Date
6 November 2026
Time
09:00–10:00
Venue
Hovedbiblioteket, plan 2

The Ordkalotten festival invites you to an open poetics seminar at the Main Library. Teachers from the Writing Programme in Tromsø share their thoughts on their own writing practice.

Kristin Vego talks about working on a novel in progress, a novel that is not finished and that may never be finished, and about the thoughts that come up along the way.
