Det er Ales – opening performance

Jon Fosse’s poetic story of grief and life after loss returns to Hålogaland Teater.

Date
19 November 2026
Time
19:00
Venue
Hålogaland Teater, Scene Øst
Address

Signe lives on in the house she shared with the man who never returned from the sea. Jon Fosse’s story explores grief, memory and the question of how to go on living after such a loss.

This is the first performance in Hålogaland Teater’s Tromsø run, at Scene Øst.
