GLIMT 2026 – Det Lille Kulturhus

An outdoor photo exhibition about Det Lille Kulturhus and Tromsø's music and youth scene.

GLIMT is the museum's annual outdoor exhibition for the dark season, projected on a large wall. This year's selection features photographs documenting life at Det Lille Kulturhus in the 1990s and 2000s.

The exhibition opening starts at 20:00, with free hot drinks and short talks. The outdoor event is free and open to everyone; dress for the weather.
