Kiuryaq – an immersive circumpolar theatre performance

A circumpolar theatre performance about the Northern Lights, belonging and stories from the North, followed by a conversation.

Archived event

Date
10 November 2026
Time
19:00
Venue
Provisoriet, Rådstua Teaterhus

Kiuryaq weaves together theatre, music and video design in a work created in collaboration between Indigenous and non-Indigenous artists from Canada, Greenland, Sápmi and Alaska. After the performance, creators Reneltta Arluk and Rawdna Carita Eira will talk with moderator Ida Løken Valkeapää. The performance is in English.
