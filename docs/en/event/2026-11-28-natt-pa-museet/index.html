Night at the Museum

An after-hours museum evening with activities for children and families, an exhibition opening and a concert.

Date
28 November 2026
Time
16:00–23:00
Venue
Norges arktiske universitetsmuseum
Address
Lars Thøringsveg 10, Tromsø
Price
Voksen 130 kr; honnør/studenter/vernepliktige 100 kr; barn under 18 år og UiT-studenter gratis

Experience the museum after closing time on Saturday 28 November. *Night at the Museum* is screened free for children and their caregivers from 16:00 to 18:00. From 17:00 to 20:00, families can explore the exhibitions with a flashlight and see whether the stories, animals and research come to life.

At 20:00, the outdoor photo exhibition GLIMT 2026 opens; the opening is free and open to everyone. At 21:00, Karlsøy Prestegaard and Verlos Vintage play in the Museum Hall.

The film screening is free for children and caregivers; children under 18 also have free admission to the exhibitions. Adults need a museum ticket for the indoor activities; tickets are sold at the museum desk on the day. The museum closes at 23:00.
