Karlsøy Prestegaard and Verlos Vintage play at the Arctic University Museum after the opening of GLIMT 2026. The exhibition photographs document the music scene around Det Lille Kulturhus, where both bands were active.

The concert is for adults and starts at 21:00. A regular museum ticket is required and is sold at the desk on the day. Unnumbered seats can be reserved at the desk from 19:00. The museum lists regular adult admission at NOK 130, senior citizens/students/military conscripts at NOK 100, and free admission for UiT students and children under 18.
