Hivju and The Garbage King is a musical collaboration between Kristofer Hivju and producer and musician Olav Lystrup. The duo describe their sound as raw, playful and richly evocative, pairing strong storytelling with a lyrical, ramshackle ensemble.

On this acoustic tour, the music is presented in a stripped-back format with double bass and nylon-string guitar. Doors open at 8:00 pm; the concert starts at 9:00 pm.
