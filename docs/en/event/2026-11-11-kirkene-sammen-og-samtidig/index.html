Churches Together Against Modern Slavery

A free Global Week gathering with music, prayer, an appeal walk and a Global Café at Tromsø Cathedral.

Tromsø Cathedral and the Salvation Army invite the public to mark Global Week, focusing on modern slavery and the Transparency Act. The programme includes music, a prayer-and-appeal walk, a short devotional talk, and a Global Café serving homemade baked goods and soup. The event is free.
