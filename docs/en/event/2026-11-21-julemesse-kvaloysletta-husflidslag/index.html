Christmas fair with Kvaløysletta Husflidslag

Handmade crafts, Christmas baking and a café at the Arctic University Museum.

Kvaløysletta Husflidslag's annual fair offers items in wood, ceramics and leather, woven, knitted and felted goods, yarn, flatbread and Christmas baking.

Everyone is welcome. Admission to the fair and café is free; a separate ticket is required for the museum exhibitions.

Saturday 21 November, 11:00–16:00, and Sunday 22 November, 11:00–15:00.
