Sindre Olav Fredriksen invites guests on stage for tall tales, anecdotes, jokes and stories. Rorbuashow blends Northern Norwegian humour and storytelling traditions with stand-up, music and conversation.

2026 is the show's final year before the group moves on to new projects. The performers are Sindre Olav Fredriksen, Borghild Aasebøstøl, Sverre Hjelle, Andreas Gregersen, Truls Nilsen and Karl Holte Aarø.
