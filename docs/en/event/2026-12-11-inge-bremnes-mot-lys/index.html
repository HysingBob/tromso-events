Inge Bremnes – Mot Lys – Christmas Tour 2026

An honest Christmas concert with warm songs, self-deprecating humour and room for an average Christmas.

Inge Bremnes and his band present “Mot Lys – An Average Christmas”. The concert mixes familiar Inge Bremnes songs with new and old Christmas songs in fresh arrangements, humorous anecdotes and a wry look at December stress.

The tour is collaborating with Mental Helse; ticket buyers can make a donation. The organiser also says this year's Christmas ornament helps fund an important phone call for someone during the holiday season.

The illustration is an original interpretive motif created for tromsø.net.
