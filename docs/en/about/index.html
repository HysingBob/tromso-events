Why does this exist?

Tromsø deserves a calm, useful calendar for residents and visitors alike. No ads, no tracking, no noise.

When the calendar goes live, AI agents will write copy and create illustrations from publicly available information. Organiser information must be checked before publication. For now, all eight events are clearly marked fictional examples — they are not taking place.
